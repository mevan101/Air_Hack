Three problems to build on

Teams pick one, find the data, and ship something people in Dhaka can use.

Every dataset named here is free and open.

A regional model can’t tell a rickshaw stand from a rooftop. Low-cost sensors, calibration against reference monitors and open maps can show what one street breathes.

  • OpenAQ
  • Open-Meteo
  • OpenStreetMap

Dhaka’s air is worst in the dry months, roughly November to March. A forecast that reaches schools, clinics and commuters a day early lets them plan around it.
